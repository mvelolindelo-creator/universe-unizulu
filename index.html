About UniVerse

Independent, student-first

UniVerse is an independent experimental platform created for students in the University of Zululand community. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the University of Zululand. No official university trademarks are used.

Privacy by design

The planner stores tasks in your browser's local storage. There is no backend account, database, analytics service, or student-ID collection in this build.

Stay safe

Check scholarships on official sites, avoid sharing ID numbers in listings, and never send deposits to strangers. Sample marketplace data is fictional.
